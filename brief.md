# Project brief

## Client and purpose

TT Education Plus — a one-person tutoring centre run by **Teacher Thomas**
("Tt"), established 2014, branded as the "Tt Learning System". Offers exam
preparation, 1-to-1 tutoring, and door-to-door (home visit) tutoring. The site
should present the courses offered and the tutoring approach, and make it easy
for visitors to get in touch (WhatsApp, Instagram @ttedu.plus).

Subjects taught (for the course/subject cards): Science, Chemistry, Physics,
Sejarah (History), Mathematics, and Bahasa Melayu (BM) — i.e. Malaysian
secondary-school / SPM-level exam preparation.

## Site type

Landing — a single-scroll home page composed of several sections, with a small
amount of multi-page/anchor navigation. Not a blog or portfolio.

## Audience

Parents (looking for a tutor for their child), students, and adult learners.
Primary desired action: browse the courses/approach, then contact via WhatsApp
or Instagram.

## Pages and sections

Primary: a single scrolling home page with anchored sections.

- Hero (name, tagline, primary WhatsApp/Instagram contact)
- Courses / subjects offered (placeholder details, drafted by Claude)
- How tutoring works (exam prep, 1-to-1, door-to-door)
- Gallery
- Schedule
- FAQs
- About
- Contact (WhatsApp + Instagram, no form)

## Content sources

Claude to draft placeholder course details and copy. Client will provide real
copy, course specifics, and the logo later. Brand colours and fonts: Claude's
choice for now.

## Tone and voice

Clean, formal, modern. Trustworthy. Avoid: anything cluttered or overly playful.

## Visual references

Liked: https://chronotasks.vercel.app/ , https://attio.com/ ,
https://www.jasper.ai/ , https://www.podia.com/
Shared DNA: light backgrounds, large bold headlines, generous whitespace, soft
rounded cards with subtle shadows, one restrained accent colour, pill buttons.

## Brand identity

Logo: a black circular badge with a large "Tt" monogram, "Teacher Thomas / IN
PROGRESS" and the year "2014", with a small yellow accent inside. Brand palette:
black + white with a yellow accent. (Real logo to be supplied later; use a
placeholder badge that matches this look.)

## Must-haves and must-nots

Must: WhatsApp link, Instagram link (@ttedu.plus), gallery, schedule, FAQs,
logo placeholder, a yellow accent somewhere in the design.
Must not: contact form, blog, projects/portfolio sections.

## Motion

Light / subtle — gentle fade and slide-in on scroll, nothing cinematic.

## Technical

Languages: English (main), Malay, Chinese — trilingual (i18n enabled).
Form endpoint: none. Analytics: none provided. Domain: TBD. Deadline: none.
