# Project brief

## Client and purpose

TT Education⁺ — a Malaysia-based private tutoring centre (Teacher Thomas, "Tt"),
established 2014, for secondary students aged 13+. Teaching formats are
**one-to-one, small-group, and online** tuition. The primary goal is to build
parents' trust and convert visitors into WhatsApp enquiries; the main CTA is
**"Book a 30-Minute Trial Class"** (deep-links to WhatsApp). Contact is WhatsApp
and Instagram (@ttedu.plus) — no contact form.

Programmes (by tier):

- **Lower Secondary** (Form 1–3): Bahasa Melayu, Mathematics, Science, Sejarah.
- **Upper Secondary** (Form 4–5 / SPM): Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Science,
  Sejarah.
- **IGCSE**: Chemistry, Physics.

(Updated per the client's confirmed brief; supersedes the earlier door-to-door
framing. EN launches first, with BM and 中文 added progressively.)

## Site type

Landing — a single-scroll home page composed of several sections, with a small
amount of multi-page/anchor navigation. Not a blog or portfolio.

## Audience

Parents (looking for a tutor for their child), students, and adult learners.
Primary desired action: browse the courses/approach, then contact via WhatsApp
or Instagram.

## Pages and sections

Primary: a single scrolling home page with anchored sections.

- Hero (name, tagline, primary "Book a 30-Minute Trial Class" WhatsApp CTA)
- How tutoring works (one-to-one, small-group, online)
- Programmes / subjects by tier (Lower Secondary, Upper Secondary, IGCSE)
- Gallery
- Testimonials (placeholder quotes until the client supplies real ones)
- Arrangements & fees ("message on WhatsApp for pricing")
- Trial-class CTA band
- FAQs
- About (tutor profile — qualifications placeholder)
- Contact (WhatsApp + Instagram, no form)

## Content sources

Claude to draft placeholder course details and copy. The client has supplied the
logo (rebuilt as an inline SVG badge) and the confirmed programme/format brief;
real testimonials, fee figures, tutor qualifications and the WhatsApp number are
still to come. Brand colours and fonts: Claude's choice (yellow accent matches
the logo's `#ffcc00` ring).

## Tone and voice

Clean, formal, modern. Trustworthy. Avoid: anything cluttered or overly playful.

## Visual references

Liked: https://chronotasks.vercel.app/ , https://attio.com/ ,
https://www.jasper.ai/ , https://www.podia.com/
Shared DNA: light backgrounds, large bold headlines, generous whitespace, soft
rounded cards with subtle shadows, one restrained accent colour, pill buttons.

## Brand identity

Logo (supplied by the client): a yellow ring on a black field with a
periodic-table "Tt" symbol, the atomic number "2014", and "Teacher Thomas / IN
PROGRESS". Rebuilt as an inline SVG badge (`src/components/BrandBadge.astro`) so
it scales and adapts to dark mode. Brand palette: black + white with the logo's
yellow accent (`#ffcc00`).

## Must-haves and must-nots

Must: WhatsApp link, Instagram link (@ttedu.plus), gallery, schedule, FAQs,
logo placeholder, a yellow accent somewhere in the design.
Must not: contact form, blog, projects/portfolio sections.

## Motion

Light / subtle — gentle fade and slide-in on scroll, nothing cinematic.

## Technical

Languages: English (main), Malay, Chinese — trilingual (i18n enabled).
Form endpoint: none. Analytics: none provided. Domain: TBD. Deadline: none.
